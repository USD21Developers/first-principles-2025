---
name: Legacy study sources
description: Source fidelity when adding or revising studies in the Legacy section.
---

Treat the supplied legacy PDF as the source for its study's wording, outline, and diagrams. Do not silently replace it with the current version of the same study.

**Why:** The user requested a distinct Legacy section and supplied the original Light And Darkness study PDF, rather than asking to replace the current study.

**How to apply:** Keep legacy and current studies separate. Preserve the original diagrams and transcribe readable text as HTML; ask before substantively revising the source's teaching or wording.
